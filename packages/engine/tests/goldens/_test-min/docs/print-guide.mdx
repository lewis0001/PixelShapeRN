All of Test Min's printed parts are designed to print **without
supports**: no overhang tricks, no rafts, no glue. Any well-tuned FDM printer
with a 0.4 mm nozzle can produce them.

## Settings

Use the `pla-0.20` profile that ships with this robot, or dial in the same
values by hand:

- **Layer height:** 0.2 mm
- **Walls / perimeters:** 3
- **Infill:** 15% gyroid
- **Supports:** none — every part is support-free by design
- Print each part flat on the bed in its default orientation.


## Parts

| Part | Qty | Material | Color role | Filament (per part) | Time (per part) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `plate` | 1 | PLA | body | 12.50 g | 66 min |

The **color role** column tells you which filament color each part uses: keep
all `body` parts one color and all `accent` parts another, and the robot will
look the way it does on the site — but this is your build, so any palette goes.

## Totals

- Filament: about **12.50 g** across all parts.
- Print time: about **66 minutes** of printing.

When everything is off the bed, check each part against the photos in the
[assembly guide](./assemble/), then move on to [wiring](./wiring/).
