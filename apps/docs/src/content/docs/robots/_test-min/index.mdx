|               |                  |
| ------------- | ---------------- |
| Difficulty    | beginner         |
| Build time    | about 10 minutes |

## What you'll build

Test Min is a robot you make yourself: you print its plastic parts,
order a handful of low-cost electronic modules, screw and press everything
together, then flash the firmware straight from your browser. When it wakes up
you drive it from your phone and teach it new tricks with a block-based
behavior builder — no soldering station or programming experience required.

## How this guide works

Work through the pages in order — each one is a short, self-contained step:

1. [Safety first](./safety/) — read this before anything else.
2. [Source the parts](./source-parts/) — everything to buy, with links.
3. [Print the parts](./print-guide/) — settings and estimates for each piece.
4. [Wire it up](./wiring/) — the wiring diagram and wire list.
5. [Assemble](./assemble/) — step-by-step, LEGO style.
6. [Flash the firmware](./flash/) — from your browser, no tools to install.
7. [First run](./first-run/) — get Test Min on your Wi-Fi and drive it.
8. [Play and program](./play/) — build behaviors with blocks.
9. [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting/) — when something doesn't work.
